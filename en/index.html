FAQ

Still have questions?

The questions we're asked most before starting a project.

How long does it take to build a website?
A landing page or one-page site is typically ready within 1 to 2 weeks once we receive your final text and images. A multi-page site usually takes between 2 and 4 weeks.
Do I need to pay a monthly fee?
No. The project is a one-time payment, with no mandatory monthly fees, and we offer the first year of maintenance. Recurring costs only apply if you choose to continue with maintenance after that period, sign up for social media management, or get your own domain name.
How much does a website cost?
The price depends on the type of project and on what you need, so we don't work with fixed prices. Request a free quote, tell us what you have in mind and we'll present you with the terms of the service.
I already have a domain, can you use it?
Yes. We connect your existing domain to the new site. If you don't have one yet, we can help you choose and register one.
Can I edit the site myself once it's live?
Yes, for simple changes like text, photos, or opening hours. We deliver clearly commented code and explain, in plain language, where and how to edit it. For bigger changes, we're available.
Will the site show up on Google?
Every site we build is already optimised for search engines: semantic structure, fast loading, well-organised text and structured data. That's the foundation of good SEO. Ranking well for competitive searches may need ongoing content work, which we can also support.
Do you build online stores?
Our focus is institutional and presentation websites, without shopping carts or payment systems. If your business needs an online store, we're happy to discuss the right solution for you.
How do we get started?
Fill in the form or contact us by email or Instagram. We'll talk about your business, propose a visual direction and a timeline, and only move forward once everything is aligned.
