FAQ

Still have questions?

The questions we're asked most before starting a project.

How long does it take to build a website or digital solution?
It depends on the type and size of the project, the content required and the time needed for approval. Once we understand what you need, we'll give you a realistic timeline before moving forward.
Will the domain be registered in my name?
Yes. If you buy your own domain, it is registered in your name. You can use a domain you already own, or ask us to help you choose and register a new one.
Do I need to buy a domain for a menu or a guide?
Not necessarily. You can use your own domain or an address associated with DD Digital, subject to the applicable terms and annual fee.
Can I edit the website or digital solution myself once it's ready?
Our solutions are custom-built and, at present, do not include a self-service editing area for clients. If you need to change text, images, opening hours, prices or other information, you can ask us to make those changes, in line with the terms of the service you've signed up for.
I already have a domain. Can you connect it to the new website?
Yes. We can use your current domain and connect it to the new website, biolink or digital solution.
Does social media management guarantee more followers or sales?
No. Social media management helps develop your brand's presence and communication, but it does not guarantee an increase in followers, enquiries or sales. Results depend on several factors, including your sector, your audience, the content and engagement with your community.
Do I have to provide the text and images?
Not necessarily! We can help you prepare the text and select images suited to your business. If you already have text, photographs or other materials, we can also include them in the project.
Do the websites include search engine optimisation (SEO)?
Yes! Our websites are built with SEO best practice in mind, including an organised page structure, suitable titles and meta descriptions, and technical aspects that help search engines understand and index the content. The optimisation is adapted to the structure and characteristics of each project.
What happens when the maintenance period ends?
At the end of the contracted period, you can renew maintenance to keep benefiting from our support, updates and the backups included in the plan. If you choose not to renew, the website can stay online as long as hosting and domain remain active, but it will no longer benefit from the maintenance services included in the plan.
How does payment work? Can I pay in instalments?
To start the project, we ask for an initial payment of 50% of the total amount upon acceptance of the quote. The remaining 50% is paid on delivery of the project. If you'd prefer to split the payment differently, we can consider that, depending on the project and the agreed terms.
